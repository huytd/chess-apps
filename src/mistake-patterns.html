How it works. Enter your username and pick how many recent games to look at. Each of your moves is checked by Stockfish the way WhyBlunder does it: the engine's top three moves before your move, its best reply after it, and on every mistake a check of what the opponent was threatening.

Every mistake and blunder is then sorted by what went wrong on the board (a piece left hanging, a fork you walked into, a tactic you missed, a threat you ignored…) and by its circumstances (time trouble, moving too fast, letting a win slip). The patterns that show up in several games are the ones to work on.

Analysis takes about 20 seconds per game (less on a fast computer, or with Quick in Settings). Analysed games are saved in this browser, so running again only checks new ones.
