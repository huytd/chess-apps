How it works

The page is searched for chessboards, and each square of each diagram is read by the small neural network from Watch Chess in 3D. Nothing is uploaded. Photos work best taken square-on, in good light, with the whole diagram in the frame.

Diagram reading from Watch Chess in 3D's square classifier; evaluations by Stockfish.js (GPLv3); PDFs opened with PDF.js. All in your browser.

Drag the four corners onto the corners of the board's squares (inside the frame).
